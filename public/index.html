Nion

Tap the button to turn Nion on.

Then say “Hey Nion” and ask out loud, for example: “What are some places to eat near me?”

How Nion works
  1. Tap Turn on Nion and allow the microphone.
  2. Say “Hey Nion” and ask anything out loud. Nion answers out loud.
  3. Talk at any time to interrupt it. Earphones work best.
  4. Say “End the conversation” when you're done. History keeps a transcript.

Who does what: Chrome turns your voice into text and speaks the answers. Claude Sonnet 5.5 writes each answer and searches the web. Claude Haiku 5.5 only helps find your city if the map lookup fails.
